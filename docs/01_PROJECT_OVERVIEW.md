# 1. Executive Summary

Room-Bot Service is a modern Hostel Service Management Platform designed to digitize, automate, and simplify hostel maintenance and service operations within educational institutions.

The platform provides a centralized environment where students can easily request hostel-related services, staff members can efficiently manage assigned tasks, and administrators can monitor the complete workflow through an intelligent dashboard.

Unlike conventional hostel management systems that rely heavily on manual registers, phone calls, WhatsApp groups, or physical complaint books, Room-Bot Service introduces an automated workflow driven by secure authentication, intelligent request assignment, OTP-based completion verification, workload balancing, and performance analytics.

The objective of this project is not merely to develop another web application, but to engineer a production-ready software solution capable of solving real operational challenges commonly faced in university hostels.

Every feature included in the platform has been carefully selected to provide practical value while ensuring scalability, maintainability, security, and an exceptional user experience.

This project is designed using modern software engineering principles and follows industry-standard architecture, making it suitable for real-world deployment after appropriate infrastructure configuration.

# 2. Project Introduction

## Overview

Room-Bot Service is a full-stack web application that acts as a bridge between students, hostel staff, and hostel administrators.

The application enables students to submit service requests digitally instead of depending upon traditional manual communication methods.

Examples of hostel services include:

- Room Cleaning
- Electrical Maintenance
- Plumbing
- AC Maintenance
- Furniture Repair

Each request submitted by a student is automatically assigned to an appropriate staff member based on predefined business rules.

The assigned staff receives the request in their dashboard, completes the assigned work, and finally verifies the completion using a secure OTP generated by the system.

The administrator has complete visibility over every user, every request, every complaint, every staff member, and overall hostel operations through a centralized administrative dashboard.

This workflow ensures transparency, accountability, fairness, and operational efficiency.

# 3. Background

Hostel maintenance is one of the most common operational challenges faced by educational institutions.

In many universities, students report issues through informal communication channels such as:

- Phone calls
- Personal visits
- WhatsApp messages
- Complaint registers
- Verbal communication

These approaches introduce several limitations.

Students often have no visibility regarding whether their complaint has been registered, assigned, or resolved.

Similarly, hostel staff frequently receive tasks without any structured assignment process, leading to unequal workload distribution and reduced efficiency.

Administrators are also unable to effectively monitor hostel operations due to the absence of centralized records and meaningful analytics.

As universities continue to digitize academic operations, hostel maintenance systems should also evolve toward modern software-driven workflows.

Room-Bot Service is designed to fulfill this requirement.

# 5. Problems in the Existing System

The traditional hostel service management process suffers from numerous operational, administrative, and communication-related issues. These challenges reduce efficiency, increase service delays, and negatively impact the overall hostel experience for both students and staff.

The following sections describe the major problems observed in the existing system.

---

## 5.1 Manual Complaint Registration

In many institutions, students are required to physically visit the hostel office or contact hostel authorities through phone calls or messaging applications to report issues.

This process has several disadvantages:

- Complaints may not be recorded properly.
- Information can be misunderstood or forgotten.
- Multiple complaints become difficult to organize.
- There is no centralized database for future reference.
- Students cannot verify whether their complaint has actually been registered.

As the number of students increases, manual complaint handling becomes increasingly inefficient.

---

## 5.2 Lack of Request Tracking

Once a complaint is submitted, students usually have no visibility regarding its progress.

Common questions include:

- Has my request been assigned?
- Who is working on it?
- When will it be completed?
- Is it still pending?
- Was it accidentally forgotten?

The absence of a tracking mechanism often creates confusion and unnecessary communication between students and hostel authorities.

---

## 5.3 Unequal Work Distribution

One of the biggest operational challenges in manual systems is unfair workload distribution.

Without an automated assignment process:

- Some staff members receive too many requests.
- Others remain underutilized.
- Workload depends entirely on manual decisions.
- Overall efficiency decreases.

An intelligent workload balancing mechanism is therefore essential.

---

## 5.4 No Verification of Completed Work

In traditional systems, once staff members claim that maintenance has been completed, there is usually no official verification process.

This creates several problems:

- Work may be marked as completed without student confirmation.
- Students may still be facing the same issue.
- Administrators have no reliable evidence that services were actually delivered.

A secure completion verification mechanism is necessary to improve accountability.

---

## 5.5 No Performance Evaluation

Since manual systems rarely collect structured feedback, administrators cannot accurately evaluate staff performance.

Without measurable performance indicators:

- Outstanding staff cannot be recognized.
- Poor service quality remains unnoticed.
- Promotions or workload decisions become subjective.
- Continuous improvement becomes difficult.

A structured rating and feedback system helps build transparency and accountability.

---

## 5.6 Poor Administrative Visibility

Hostel administrators often struggle to answer important operational questions such as:

- How many requests are currently pending?
- Which hostel block has the highest number of complaints?
- Which staff member has the maximum workload?
- Which services consume the most resources?
- How quickly are requests being resolved?

Without centralized data, these insights are impossible to generate.

---

## 5.7 Lack of Historical Records

Manual records are often incomplete, difficult to search, and vulnerable to loss.

Consequences include:

- Difficulty investigating previous complaints.
- No request history.
- No audit trail.
- No long-term analysis.
- Reduced transparency.

A centralized digital history significantly improves operational management.

---

# 6. Proposed Solution

Room-Bot Service addresses the limitations of traditional hostel service management by introducing a secure, centralized, and fully digital platform.

The platform automates every major stage of the hostel service lifecycle, from request creation to completion verification and performance analysis.

Instead of depending on manual communication, every interaction is recorded within the system, ensuring transparency and accountability.

The proposed solution focuses on five major goals:

1. Digitalization
2. Automation
3. Security
4. Transparency
5. Scalability

---

## 6.1 Digital Service Requests

Students can submit hostel service requests directly through the web application without contacting hostel authorities manually.

Each request includes:

- Service Category
- Description
- Block
- Room Number
- Submission Time
- Current Status

The request is immediately stored in the database for further processing.

---

## 6.2 Intelligent Staff Assignment

Unlike traditional manual assignment, Room-Bot Service automatically assigns requests based on predefined business rules.

Assignment considers:

- Student Block
- Staff Block
- Staff Role
- Current Active Workload

If multiple staff members satisfy all conditions, the system randomly selects one among those with the minimum active workload.

This approach ensures fair workload distribution and eliminates administrative bias.

---

## 6.3 OTP-Based Work Completion

To ensure that services are genuinely completed, Room-Bot Service introduces OTP verification.

The workflow is as follows:

1. Staff finishes the assigned work.
2. The system generates a secure OTP.
3. OTP is sent to the student's registered email.
4. Student provides the OTP to the staff member after verifying the work.
5. Staff enters the OTP.
6. System verifies the OTP.
7. Request status changes to **Completed**.

This prevents false completion reports and improves trust between students and hostel management.

---

## 6.4 Performance Evaluation Through Feedback

After successful completion of a request, students are allowed to submit feedback.

Feedback consists of:

- Rating (1–5 Stars)
- Optional Written Review

The submitted ratings contribute to the staff member's overall performance score, enabling administrators to monitor service quality objectively.

---

## 6.5 Administrative Dashboard

The administrator dashboard provides centralized control over the entire system.

Major capabilities include:

- Student Management
- Staff Management
- Request Monitoring
- Complaint Monitoring
- Analytics Dashboard
- Staff Performance Tracking
- Block-wise Statistics
- Service-wise Statistics

The dashboard enables administrators to make informed operational decisions using real-time data.

# 18. High-Level System Architecture

The application follows a three-tier architecture.

```text
                Student
                  │
                Staff
                  │
               Administrator
                  │
        ───────────────────────
              React Frontend
        ───────────────────────
                  │
             REST API (HTTPS)
                  │
        ───────────────────────
          Express.js Backend
        ───────────────────────
                  │
      Authentication Middleware
                  │
           Business Logic Layer
                  │
          PostgreSQL Database
```

This architecture separates the presentation layer, business logic layer, and data layer, improving maintainability and scalability.
